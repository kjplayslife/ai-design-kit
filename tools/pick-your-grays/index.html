A one-decision guide

Pick Your Grays

Pick one dark gray. Everything else comes from it: a 12-step scale, the jobs each step does, and a light mode you tune at the bottom. The page re-themes itself as you go.

Step 1

Pick your dark gray

This is your page background. Start from an app you like, then adjust. Rules: not black (3–8% lightness), a hint of hue at 3–10% saturation.

Cool → 220–250. Neutral → 240 at very low saturation. Warm → 20–40. You can match it to your brand in Step 3.
3–10% reads as gray. Above about 12% you can name the color, and it's too much. Judge it by the middle of the scale below, not this dark swatch.
Not black. 3–8%. 0% is #000 and makes everything float. 10–14% is “lifted”, like Lovable and Discord.

    Step 2

    Your 12-step dark scale

    Generated from your gray. Same hue and saturation on every step, only lightness changes. Lighter = closer to you. Each step has one job. Click a step to nudge its lightness.

    Step 3

    Pick your brand color

    One color, for buttons, links, and the one thing on each screen that matters most. You're picking how it looks on dark. Light mode derives a darker version and you can nudge it at the bottom.

    Or skip this and use the sliders.
    Blue / indigo (220–250) reads as trust. Green / teal (150–180) reads as growth. Violet (270) and orange (20) read as energy. Skip 45–95 (yellow, lime) and 0–12 (red).
    Brand colors are 60–95% saturated. Under about 25% it becomes a neutral brand: a white button on dark, a black button on light.
    On dark, 50–65% pops without going neon. Pull saturation back a little if it glows.
    Sets the Step 1 hue to this hue, at 3–10% saturation.

      Step 5

      Light mode, generated

      A 12-step light scale from the same hue and saturation, with the jobs mirrored: near-white page, white cards with a border and a small shadow, your brand a bit darker so white text passes. Tune only what looks off.

      Light scale, steps 1–12. Step 1 is the page, 12 is text.
      Derived as your dark brand, 8 points darker and 10% more saturated. Slide left if white text on it is faint.
      11 is the low-contrast text step. 10 is lighter; use it only for captions that sit next to strong text.
      6 is the subtle border. 7 if cards need to hold their edge on busy pages.
      3 is the normal fill for inputs and rows. 2 is barely there.
